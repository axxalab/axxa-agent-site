What it does

Three modes in one panel, designed for the Obsidian mobile drawer first and the same on desktop.

Answers that cite your notes

Vault Q&A runs hybrid search (keyword and vector, re-ranked by your links) and cites the notes it used as wikilinks you can open.

An agent that asks first

It creates, edits, moves and deletes notes through a small set of tools. Changes wait for your OK, deletes go to your trash, and moves rewrite your links.

Made for your phone

The composer follows the keyboard, there is an optional fullscreen mode, and the agent works on the phone too.

Six providers, your keys

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM and local Ollama. Keys live in your OS keychain.

Small and fast

About 440 KB of JavaScript, against a median of about 3.8 MB for the 12 most-downloaded AI plugins (measured October 2026).

Everything is Markdown

Chats, skills and cost reports are files in your vault, and a usage dashboard shows what you spend by provider, model and day.

Get started in a minute

Requires Obsidian 1.11.4 or newer, on desktop or mobile.

  1. Open the AXXA Agent page and choose Add to Obsidian, or search for AXXA Agent in Settings → Community plugins → Browse.
  2. Install and enable it, then open Settings → AXXA Agent → Providers.
  3. Add a key for one provider, or the address of your Ollama server (usually http://localhost:11434), and start a chat.

Free ways to start

You only need one provider.

  • Google Gemini has a free tier.
  • OpenRouter has free models, marked in the model picker.
  • NVIDIA NIM gives free credits.
  • Ollama runs models on your own computer, with no key and no account.

Your notes stay yours

No telemetry, no account, nothing sent to us.

Keys on your device

Stored in your OS keychain through Obsidian's secret storage, never in the plugin's settings file, so they don't travel through Sync or backups.

Only the provider you chose

Your notes leave the device only to the AI provider you picked, as part of a conversation or the Vault Q&A index. The index itself stays in your vault.

Offline with Ollama

Chat and the agent run fully on your machine with a local model; Vault Q&A then searches by keyword, because the embedding models are cloud services.

Every network request the plugin makes is listed in the Disclosures. This site's own practices are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature in the plugin today is free, with no tier, no account and no license key, and it stays that way. If paid options appear later, they will be new things built on top, never a lock on something that already worked. You pay your AI provider directly; the plugin takes no cut.

Does the agent work on mobile?

Yes. Chat, Vault Q&A and the agent with its confirmations all run in the Obsidian mobile app. Cloud providers work anywhere; Ollama runs on a computer, so using it from a phone needs an Ollama server the phone can reach.

What leaves my device?

Only what goes to the provider you chose: your messages, what you attach, and the note excerpts that Vault Q&A and the agent use. Nothing goes to us.

Can I keep other AI plugins installed?

Yes. AXXA runs in its own panel, its styles are scoped to that panel, and it doesn't depend on or replace other plugins.

Where do I report a bug or ask a question?

Bugs go to the bug report form, questions and ideas to Discussions.
